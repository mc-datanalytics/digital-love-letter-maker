## 💌 Why Digital Love Letters?

Traditional paper cards are fragile, easy to misplace, and static. A **digital love letter** created on [Heartreveals](https://heartreveals.com) transforms your words into an interactive story:

- ✉️ **Interactive Wax-Seal Opening**: An envelope animation that gently opens upon touch or click.
- 🖋️ **Personalized Handwriting Fonts**: Choose from elegant calligraphy styles (*Dancing Script*, *Great Vibes*, *Parisienne*).
- 📸 **Private Photo Memories**: High-resolution gallery with soft fades and captions.
- ⏳ **Midnight & Countdown Reveal**: Deliver a surprise that stays locked until midnight in your partner's timezone.
- 🎟️ **Digital Scratch Cards**: Hidden coupons (*"Free weekend trip"*, *"A home-cooked dinner"*, *"One secret wish"*).
- 🔒 **Privacy-First**: Delivered exclusively through a private encrypted link or custom QR code.

## 🚀 How to Create a Love Note Online

1. Head over to the **[Heartreveals Creation Wizard](https://heartreveals.com/create.html)**.
2. Select your aesthetic palette (e.g. Parisian Rose, Starlight Midnight, Golden Sunset).
3. Write your letter, upload up to 8 favorite photos, and choose romantic background audio.
4. Set an unlock date or instant access.
5. Share the secret link via text, WhatsApp, or print the generated QR code inside a gift box.
